Block Builder Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PlayAct Games ("we", "us") handles information when you play Block Builder (the "Game") on Android. We do not run servers for the Game, there are no user accounts, and we do not sell personal information. The Game shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which collects some information from your device as described below, and offers optional in-app purchases handled by Google Play.

1. Information stored on your device

The Game saves a small amount of data only on your device: your best score, your settings (sound, music, vibration), your screws balance, whether you own Remove Ads, the block styles and power-ups you own, and a record of the Google Play purchase codes already delivered (so a purchase is never applied twice). We do not receive this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows banner ads, occasional full-screen ads between games, and optional rewarded ads (you choose to watch one for a second chance). If you buy Remove Ads (see section 5), banner and between-game ads are no longer requested or shown; only the optional rewarded ads remain. These ads are served by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising ID, IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • ad interactions (which ads were shown or tapped) and diagnostic information.

We do not see or store this information ourselves. Google's use of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices: if you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the Game shows Google's consent form when you first open it, before any ads are loaded, asking whether your information may be used for personalised ads. If you decline, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads, which use less information (for example, to count ads and prevent fraud). Where the law requires it, the Game also shows a Privacy options button in its Settings, so you can review or change your choice at any time. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

3. Information we collect

We do not collect or receive any personal information ourselves. Apart from the advertising described above, the Game contains no analytics or other third-party tracking services. An internet connection is only needed to load ads and to make purchases; the Game can be played offline.

4. Device permissions

The Game may use your device's vibration to give a short buzz when you drop a block, and internet / network state access to load ads and connect to Google Play. The Google Mobile Ads SDK also uses the advertising ID permission, and the Google Play Billing library uses the Google Play billing permission to process purchases. None of these give us access to your personal information.

5. In-app purchases

The Game offers optional packs of screws (the in-game currency) and a one-time Remove Ads purchase for real money. Purchases are entirely optional and the whole Game can be played without them: screws can also be earned by playing, and Remove Ads only turns off ads.

  • All purchases are processed by Google Play under the Google Play Terms of Service. Google handles your payment details and purchase history as described in the Google Privacy Policy; we never receive your name, email address, card or other payment details.
  • To deliver a purchase, the Game receives from Google Play only which item you bought (for example a screw pack or Remove Ads), its status (completed or pending) and a purchase code (token). This is used only to deliver and confirm your purchase with Google Play, and to show local prices from Google Play in the shop. It is kept on your device; we do not send it to our own servers.
  • If a payment is pending (for example a cash or bank payment that hasn't cleared yet), the item is delivered once Google Play confirms the payment, the next time you open the Game.
  • Remove Ads turns off banner ads and the ads between games. Optional rewarded ads (which you choose to watch for a second chance) remain available. Because Google Play keeps this purchase on your Google account, the Game checks it with Google Play when it starts, so it is restored automatically if you reinstall the Game or use another device with the same account, and it is removed if the purchase is refunded.
  • Screws you buy are stored on your device. Because there are no accounts, they cannot be transferred to another device and are lost if you uninstall the Game or clear its data. If a purchase was paid for but not delivered, use Shop → Restore purchases; this also re-checks Remove Ads.
  • You can require password or biometric confirmation for every purchase in the Play Store app (Settings → Authentication → Require authentication for purchases); parents can also use Google Family Link to approve purchases.
  • For refunds or billing questions, use Google Play (Google Play refund policies) or contact us.

6. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the Game are limited to content suitable for general and family audiences (rated PG or lower). In-app purchases are made through Google Play, which applies its own age and parental-approval settings. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, or made a purchase without permission, contact us and we will help.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Game data (scores, settings, screws, items and delivered-purchase records; purchased screws are lost too, while Remove Ads is restored from your Google account the next time you install the Game): uninstall the Game or clear its data in your device settings (Settings → Apps → Block Builder → Storage → Clear data).
  • Payment and purchase-history data is held by Google Play; you can view it in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history.
  • Advertising data is held by Google; you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings, and manage your Google data at myaccount.google.com.
  • We hold no other data about you, so there is nothing else to delete.

For any request or question, email us and we will respond as soon as possible.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
