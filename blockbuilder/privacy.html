Block Builder Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PlayAct Games ("we", "us") handles information when you play Block Builder (the "Game") on Android. In short: the Game does not collect, send or share any personal information. We do not run servers for the Game, there are no user accounts, and we do not sell personal information.

1. Information stored on your device

The Game saves a small amount of data only on your device: your best score and your sound setting (on or off). We do not receive this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

2. Information we collect

None. The Game does not collect or transmit personal information, device identifiers, location, contacts, photos or usage data. It does not contain advertising, analytics or other third-party tracking services, and it does not require an internet connection to play.

3. Device permissions

The Game may use your device's vibration to give a short buzz when you drop a block. This permission does not give us access to any of your information.

4. In-app purchases and advertising

The Game currently has no in-app purchases and no ads. If we add either in a future update, we will update this policy before that update is released and describe what information those services use.

5. Children

Because the Game does not collect personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children. If you have any concern about a child's use of the Game, contact us and we will help.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • Game data (best score and sound setting): uninstall the Game or clear its data in your device settings (Settings → Apps → Block Builder → Storage → Clear data).
  • We hold no other data about you, so there is nothing else to delete.

For any request or question, email us and we will respond as soon as possible.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
