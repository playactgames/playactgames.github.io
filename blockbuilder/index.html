Block Builder

Stack. Repair. Climb.

Block Builder is a free, casual stacking game for Android. Blocks slide in from the side. Tap to drop each one onto your tower, and anything hanging over the edge gets sliced off. Keep your drops clean to build higher and higher, from a sleepy village at night past hot-air balloons, clouds and rockets.

  • One-tap controls – tap anywhere to drop the block.
  • Perfect streaks – land three clean drops in a row and every clean drop after that repairs your tower.
  • Combo scoring – each clean drop in a streak is worth more than the last.
  • A sky that changes as you climb – balloons, clouds, rockets and a starry night.
  • Beat your best – your high score is saved on your device.
