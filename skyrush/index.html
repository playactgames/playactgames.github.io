SkyRush

Jump. Dodge. Land.

SkyRush is a free, casual skydiving game for Android. Jump out of the plane, steer your monster through flocks of birds and floating bombs, and land safely at the bottom. Every level drops you from higher up.

  • One-finger controls – slide or hold to steer.
  • Power-ups – Speed Dive, Slow Birds and Shields.
  • Unlockable monsters and rides – umbrellas, balloons, wings, a helicopter and more.
  • Upgrades and start boosters in the shop
  • Second chances when you run out of hearts
