SkyRush Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PlayAct Games ("we", "us") handles information when you play SkyRush (the "Game") on Android. We do not run our own servers for the Game and we do not have our own user accounts. The Game uses Google services for ads, purchases, sign-in, leaderboards and cloud save, described below. We do not sell personal information.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress and settings are saved on your device: gems, upgrades, start boosters, owned and equipped monsters and rides, your best level and depth, whether you bought Remove Ads, your settings (music, sound, vibration, control mode), and a random install identifier the Game creates to combine progress from different devices correctly. This identifier is not linked to your name or to any other identifier.

If you do not sign in to Google Play Games, this data stays only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data. If you sign in, your progress is also backed up as described in section 2.

2. Google Play Games (sign-in, leaderboard and cloud save)

The Game can use Google Play Games Services. On Android, Google may sign you in automatically if you already use Play Games. Signing in is optional: you can play the full Game without it.

  • Sign-in: Google provides the Game with your Play Games player ID, gamer name and profile picture.
  • Leaderboard ("Deepest Dive"): when you are signed in, your best score is sent to Google and shown publicly on the leaderboard with your Play Games gamer name and profile picture, according to your Play Games profile visibility settings.
  • Cloud save: when you are signed in, your game progress (the data listed in section 1, except your settings and the Remove Ads status) is saved in Google Play Games Saved Games under your Google account, so it can be restored after reinstalling or on a new device.

This data is stored and processed by Google, not on our servers. You control your Play Games profile, visibility and data in the Google Play Games app and your Google account settings. See the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads served by Google AdMob, including a banner, interstitial ads and optional rewarded ads (for example, to get a second chance). To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Depending on your choices and region, ads may be personalised.

  • Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), you are asked for consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with the PRIVACY button in the Game's Settings.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads / Privacy → Ads).
  • Learn more in Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

The "Remove Ads" purchase removes the banner and interstitial ads. Rewarded ads remain available because you choose to watch them.

4. In-app purchases (Google Play)

Purchases (gem packs and Remove Ads) are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was made so we can deliver the item. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

5. App diagnostics

If you have allowed it on your device, Google Play may collect crash reports and performance data (for example, the app stopping or freezing). We see this only as aggregated, anonymous statistics in the Google Play Console and use it to fix problems.

6. Why we use this information

  • To run the Game, save your progress and deliver your purchases (needed to provide the Game you use).
  • To back up your progress and show the leaderboard, when you choose to sign in to Play Games.
  • To show ads that fund the Game; personalised ads only with your consent where the law requires it.
  • To find and fix crashes and other problems.

7. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Data retention and deletion

  • Game progress on your device: uninstall the Game or clear its data in your device settings.
  • Cloud save and leaderboard: kept by Google Play Games until you delete them. You can delete your Play Games data for the Game, or your whole Play Games profile, in the Google Play Games app (Profile → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data). Note: if you are still signed in on a device with the Game installed, the Game may back up that device's progress again.
  • Purchases: your purchase history is kept by Google Play under your Google account. Remove Ads is restored automatically when you reinstall the Game with the same account.
  • Advertising data: controlled by Google; see the links above.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EEA, the UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because we do not run our own servers, most of this data is held by Google and can be managed directly through your Google account and the tools above. For any request or question, email us and we will respond within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Security

We rely on Google's services for ads, purchases, sign-in and cloud save, which use industry-standard security. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
