HoopSwipes

Swipe. Shoot. Swish.

HoopSwipes is a free, casual basketball game for Android. Swipe the ball toward the hoop, chain swishes to build combos, catch fire and beat your best score.

  • Endless mode – one ball at a time, one miss ends the run, and the hoop starts moving.
  • Timed mode – rapid fire against the clock; reach each target to earn more time.
  • Daily missions, daily rewards and free gifts
  • Unlockable balls in the shop
  • Leaderboards with Google Play Games
